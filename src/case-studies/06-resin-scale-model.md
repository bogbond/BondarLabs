## The brief

A high-detail scale model where smooth surfaces and crisp features matter. Resin is often the best choice when you want a clean “high-definition” result at smaller scales.

## What mattered

- Surface quality and fine detail
- Clean visible faces (support strategy)
- A part delivered ready to use or paint

## How we approached it

For resin projects, the biggest quality lever is support placement and orientation. The goal is to protect the visible faces while still ensuring a reliable print.

All resin orders include a full studio workflow as standard:

**support removal → thorough washing/cleaning → controlled drying → final UV curing → QC + safe packaging**.

This process helps avoid issues like trapped moisture in cavities and ensures parts arrive safe-to-handle.

## Outcome

A crisp resin model with smooth surfaces and fine feature definition, delivered fully washed, dried and UV-cured.

## Options

- For best dimensional consistency, grey water-washable resin is often the go-to choice.
- If the part is larger, we can propose hollowing and drainage by agreement.
