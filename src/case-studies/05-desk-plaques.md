## The brief

A set of personalised plaques intended for desk or wall use. The priority is a clean look: crisp text and tidy edges that feel “finished” straight out of the box.

## What mattered

- Readable text and crisp lettering
- Predictable surface quality across multiple items
- Colour choice and a consistent look

## How we approached it

For presentation pieces, the design and print plan aim to minimise visible support marks on the front faces. PLA is a great match here thanks to its reliable detail and broad colour availability.

If a project requires a very specific aesthetic (e.g., ultra-smooth surfaces or a paint-ready finish), we can quote optional cosmetic finishing. In many cases, however, PLA prints can already be suitable for priming/painting without heavy sanding.

## Outcome

Clean, personalised plaques ready to display.

## Options

- For outdoor use, **ASA** is often a better choice.
- For a premium “painted” finish, priming/painting can be arranged by agreement.
