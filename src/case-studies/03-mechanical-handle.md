## The brief

A handle intended for repeated real-world use. The main goal is a part that feels solid in the hand and holds up to normal wear.

## What mattered

- Strength in the mounting region and along the grip
- A comfortable, consistent surface finish
- Material choice suitable for tougher environments

## How we approached it

ASA is often a strong choice when you want a functional FDM part that can handle more demanding conditions, including outdoor exposure (UV resistance) and mild heat compared to PLA.

For handles, we focus on practical print strategy: selecting orientation and wall structure so that layer lines are not placed in the weakest direction for expected loads. If the handle interfaces with hardware, we can adjust holes/clearances and optionally add features for inserts.

## Outcome

A durable handle suitable for everyday use, with a print plan that can be repeated for future orders.

## Options

- For indoor functional parts where cost is the priority, **PETG** is often an excellent alternative.
- For threaded fastening points, **heat-set inserts** can be added by agreement.
