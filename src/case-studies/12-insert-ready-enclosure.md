## The brief

A simple enclosure-style part intended to be assembled and used — not just displayed. The goal is a practical prototype that fits the internal components and can be opened/closed without fragile threads.

## What mattered

- Predictable fit for internal components
- Clean mating faces and sensible clearances
- Fast iteration without overpaying for unnecessary specs

## How we approached it

For enclosure work, CAD-based support is often the difference between a “nice model” and a part that actually assembles. We can adjust clearances, hole sizes and interfaces based on the use case, then validate fit through quick iterations when needed.

PETG is often a great material choice for functional enclosures: it’s tougher than PLA and performs well for everyday handling.

If the enclosure needs durable threaded fastening points, heat-set brass inserts can be installed by agreement.

## Outcome

A functional enclosure prototype that’s ready for assembly and iteration — with options to scale into a small batch once the design is confirmed.
