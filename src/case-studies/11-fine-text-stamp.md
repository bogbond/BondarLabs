## The brief

A stamp-style master with fine text and crisp edges. The key requirement is legibility: small features must print cleanly without softened corners.

## What mattered

- Small text that remains readable at the chosen size
- Sharp edges and consistent depth
- A clean surface suitable for use as a master in downstream workflows

## How we approached it

For fine text, resin is usually the best tool. We recommend grey resin for predictable behaviour and strong dimensional consistency, and we choose a support strategy that keeps the text face as clean as possible.

All resin orders include full processing as standard: support removal, thorough cleaning, controlled drying, final UV curing, QC and safe packaging.

## Outcome

A crisp, fully processed master with clean text and edges, ready to use as a stamp or as a master for mould-making.
