## The brief

An early-stage prototype where speed matters: the goal is to get a physical part in hand, validate fit and ergonomics, then refine the design.

## What mattered

- Fast turnaround without overpaying for unnecessary specs
- Enough accuracy to evaluate interfaces and clearances
- A print plan that can be repeated for iteration #2 and #3

## How we approached it

For rapid prototypes, PLA is often the most efficient material: it prints cleanly and is cost-effective. We select settings that balance speed and usability, then adjust if the next iteration needs more strength, better finish or tighter tolerances.

If you don’t have a perfect file, we can help with practical file support so the model prints reliably.

## Outcome

A fast prototype suitable for hands-on evaluation and iteration planning.
