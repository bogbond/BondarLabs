## The brief

A miniature-sized model where the important features are tiny: facial detail, sharp edges, and clean surfaces.

## What mattered

- Fine detail without muddy edges
- Support placement that protects the “front” view
- A finished, safe-to-handle part ready for priming and painting

## How we approached it

Miniatures are one of the strongest use cases for resin: you can achieve crisp detail and smoother surfaces than typical filament printing.

Grey water-washable resin is commonly recommended because it behaves predictably and typically gives the best dimensional consistency. White and clear resins can be used too, but may require extra tuning depending on the geometry.

After printing, resin parts go through a full workflow as standard: washing, controlled drying, final UV curing, then QC and safe packaging.

## Outcome

A crisp, high-detail miniature delivered fully processed and ready for the next step (painting, mould-making, or display).
