## The brief

A decorative print intended for display. For this type of job, the goal is usually a clean surface and a tidy “finished” look.

## What mattered

- Visible faces with minimal support marks
- A consistent surface finish
- A result suitable for display or light handling

## How we approached it

PLA is often the easiest route to a clean-looking print, especially when colour choice matters. We plan orientation and supports so the most visible faces are protected, then clean up the part carefully before dispatch.

If the goal is a paint-ready finish or a very smooth surface, optional cosmetic finishing can be quoted depending on the model.

## Outcome

A clean display piece delivered ready to use, with finish options available if you need a higher cosmetic standard.
