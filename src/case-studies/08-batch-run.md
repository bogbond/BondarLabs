## The brief

A commercial-style order where the priority is not a single perfect print — it’s **consistent output** across the batch, predictable lead times, and a smooth reorder process.

## What mattered

- Repeatable settings and stable output
- Clear expectations for finish and acceptable variation
- Packaging that matches how parts will be used downstream

## How we approached it

For batches, we confirm the essentials upfront: material, finish expectations, critical dimensions (if any), quantity, timeline, and packaging requirements. When it reduces risk, a first-article sample is produced before committing to the full run.

PETG is often a great choice for batches because it’s practical for functional parts and provides a good balance of durability and consistency.

## Outcome

A predictable batch workflow that supports repeat orders: confirmed specs, optional measurement documentation, and packaging options agreed in advance.
