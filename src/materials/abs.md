## When to use ABS

- Functional parts needing higher heat resistance
- Housings, brackets and fixtures
- Parts that may be post-processed

## Advantages

- Good toughness and temperature resistance
- Can be sanded and finished

## Considerations

ABS is less UV-resistant outdoors. If the part will live outside, **ASA** is usually preferred.
