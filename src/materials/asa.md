## When to use ASA

- Outdoor brackets, covers and housings
- Parts exposed to sunlight (UV)
- Functional prints requiring durability

## Advantages

- Excellent UV and weather resistance
- Strong mechanical properties
- Good temperature performance

## Considerations

ASA is more demanding to print than PLA/PETG, but delivers excellent results for outdoor use.
