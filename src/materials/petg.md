## When to use PETG

- Functional parts and enclosures
- Components needing extra toughness
- Parts that may see moderate heat

## Advantages

- Good strength and impact resistance
- Better temperature performance vs PLA
- Good layer adhesion

## Considerations

PETG can be slightly more flexible than ABS/ASA. For outdoor UV resistance, **ASA** is often the best option.
