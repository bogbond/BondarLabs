## When to use TPU

- Gaskets and seals
- Protective caps and bumpers
- Grips and flexible fittings

## Advantages

- Flexible and impact-absorbing
- Durable for many everyday uses

## Considerations

TPU design needs the right wall thickness and geometry. We can advise on printability and flexibility.
