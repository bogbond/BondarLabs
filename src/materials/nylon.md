## When to use Nylon

- Functional engineering components
- Parts under repeated stress
- Wear-resistant applications

## Advantages

- Excellent toughness and fatigue resistance
- Good wear resistance

## Considerations

Nylon is a more advanced filament. We’ll confirm suitability based on your geometry and requirements.
