## When to use CF composites

- Rigid brackets and fixtures
- Functional tooling and jigs
- Parts where stiffness matters

## Advantages

- Increased stiffness and dimensional stability
- Strong mechanical performance

## Considerations

Composite filaments can be more abrasive and may not be ideal for very fine details. We’ll advise based on the model.
