## When to use PLA

- Prototypes and iterative design
- Visual models and display pieces
- General parts that won’t see high heat

## Advantages

- Excellent surface finish and detail
- Cost-effective
- Easy to print and consistent results

## Considerations

PLA is not ideal for hot environments (e.g. a car dashboard). If you need higher temperature resistance, consider **PETG**, **ABS** or **ASA**.
