## Available resins

- **Water-washable (default grey)**
- **ABS-like** (tougher prototypes)
- **Flexible (F80 type)**
- **Transparent** (on request)
- Other colours available on request

## Best for

- Miniatures and detailed models
- Precision prototypes
- Parts where surface finish matters

## Notes

Resin prints can be more brittle than many FDM materials. If your part needs maximum toughness or outdoor resistance, we’ll recommend an FDM option.
