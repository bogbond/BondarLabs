## Studio-grade FDM for functional parts

FDM (Fused Deposition Modelling) is ideal when you need **strong, practical parts** quickly and cost‑effectively — from prototypes and fit checks to end‑use components. We run a **multi‑printer workflow** with consistent profiles, documented settings where needed, and **quality checks before dispatch**.

If you’re after ultra‑fine surface finish or micro‑detail, see our **[Resin 3D Printing]({{ '/services/resin/' | url }})** service.

## Best for

- Functional prototypes and iterative development
- Brackets, adapters, mounts, clips and covers
- Enclosures and housings
- Jigs, fixtures and workshop tooling
- Small‑batch production (dozens to hundreds of parts)

## Build size & large parts

For single‑piece prints we can produce parts up to **420 × 420 × 480 mm**, with smaller standard volumes available for faster turnaround. When a model exceeds a single build volume, we can **split it into sections**, add **alignment features** (tabs/pins/slots where appropriate), and then **bond or assemble** the final part. This approach keeps overall part size flexible without sacrificing fit.

## Materials & colours

We print reliably in **PLA, PETG, ABS, ASA, Nylon, TPU**, and **carbon‑filled PLA**.

For colour options, we keep a broad in‑stock range for everyday work (especially PLA and core PETG colours). Engineering materials (ABS/ASA/Nylon/TPU) are typically stocked in practical tones (black/white/natural), and we can source specific brands or colours on request.

For a deeper breakdown of material properties and recommendations, see **[Materials]({{ '/materials/' | url }})**.

## Quality, accuracy & tolerances

Most functional jobs are printed at **0.16–0.20 mm** layer height for an excellent balance of speed and finish. For finer detail we can go down to **0.06–0.12 mm**, and for faster draft parts up to **0.28–0.30 mm**.

For dimensional work, we target accuracy **up to ±0.2%** for suitable geometries and materials. Where a project requires tighter fit or repeatability, we can run **test prints and calibration** for the specific part/material combination and iterate until the fit is right — supported by measured QC on critical dimensions.

## Finishing & add‑ons

Standard post‑processing includes **support removal** and basic clean‑up. Optional services (quoted case‑by‑case) include:

- Careful support clean‑up and cosmetic finishing
- Sanding and surface preparation
- Splitting and **bonding/assembly** for large parts
- **Heat‑set brass inserts** for robust threaded fastening
- Hardware supply (from stock or sourced) and pre‑assembly checks
- Embedded **NFC chips** inside printed items (tags, keychains, accessories)
- Priming and painting (project dependent)

## Turnaround & delivery

Typical lead time for small orders is **2–3 business days**. Urgent prints can sometimes be **dispatched the same day** (subject to print time and schedule). For larger quantities (50–500+ parts), lead time is typically **3–7 days**, depending on material, settings and post‑processing.

We offer **local collection** near Cambridge, UK‑wide shipping, and international delivery on request.

## Getting a quote

Every part is different — geometry, material, settings, supports, finishing and quantity all affect cost and lead time. For an accurate quote, send your file(s) and tell us:

- What the part is for (strength / heat / appearance)
- Any critical dimensions or fit requirements
- Preferred material/colour (if known)
- Quantity and deadline

If you’re unsure about material or settings, we’ll recommend options so you don’t pay for specs you don’t need.

## File types

STL is ideal for quick quoting. We can also work with STEP and other common CAD/export formats — if you’re unsure, attach what you have and we’ll advise.
