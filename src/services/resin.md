## When resin is the right choice

Resin (MSLA) printing is the best option when you need **very fine detail**, **smooth surfaces**, and **crisp small features**. It’s ideal for miniatures, display pieces, master models for mould-making workflows, stamps/seals with small text, and precision prototypes where surface finish matters.

If your priority is **large build size**, **impact resistance**, or rugged functional parts, FDM printing may be a better fit (see **FDM 3D Printing**).

---

## Surface quality & what to expect

Resin prints deliver a clean surface straight from the process, with sharp edges and excellent detail at small scales. Where supports are required, we remove them carefully and clean up contact points to keep the surface consistent. Most parts are ready for **priming and painting** without sanding (cosmetic finishing can be quoted if you need a showcase surface).

---

## Studio workflow — included in every order

Every resin order is delivered as a finished, safe-to-handle part — not a “fresh print”.

**Our standard workflow includes:**
1. Print setup with a support strategy designed for clean surfaces and reliable success
2. Careful support removal and initial clean-up
3. Thorough washing/cleaning of the part
4. **Controlled drying** at moderate temperature (typically **50–70°C**, depending on resin and geometry)
5. Final **UV curing** in a dedicated UV chamber
6. Quality check, then **safe packaging** for shipping

This drying-first approach helps reduce the risk of defects on parts with cavities or complex geometry, where trapped moisture can cause issues if curing happens too early.

## Resin options

We stock a range of resins for different needs and can recommend the best choice based on how the part will be used.

**Water-washable (default)**  
A clean, practical workflow and reliable high-detail results. **Grey** is the go-to choice for dimensional consistency and surface quality. We also stock black, white and clear.

**Standard resin**  
A solid choice for detailed models and clean surfaces.

**ABS-like resin**  
A tougher feel for parts that need more durability (application-dependent).

**Flexible resin (on request)**  
For parts that need bend/flex. Suitability depends strongly on geometry and support strategy, so we confirm feasibility before production.

## Smart optimisation: hollowing & drainage (by agreement)

Parts can be printed solid by default for maximum reliability. For larger models, we can also propose **hollowing** to reduce resin usage and cost, adding **drainage holes** and washing internal cavities properly. If required, drainage openings can be sealed after cleaning and curing. We only apply these optimisations after explaining the trade-offs and confirming with you.

## FAQ

**Do you ship fully cured parts?**  
Yes — parts are washed, dried and UV-cured as standard before shipping.

**Can you print fine text and small details?**  
Yes — resin is ideal for crisp small features and high-definition surfaces.

**Will there be support marks?**  
Supports are removed carefully and contact points are cleaned, but support placement depends on geometry. We aim for the cleanest surface possible.

**Can you hollow a model to reduce cost?**  
Yes — by agreement. We can hollow, add drainage, and clean internal cavities properly.

**Which resin should I choose?**  
Tell us the use case and we’ll recommend the best option (water-washable, standard, ABS-like, or flexible on request).

**Do you ship across the UK?**  
Yes, UK-wide shipping is standard. International shipping is available on request.
