## Built for business: predictable output and repeatable reorders

For commercial work, the goal isn’t just “a successful print” — it’s **consistent output**, **clear specifications**, and a workflow that supports **repeat orders** without surprises. We help you move from prototype to batch production with a practical, engineering-first approach.

Whether you need robust FDM parts for fixtures and product components, or high-detail resin parts for masters, stamps and precision small components, we plan production around your priorities: cost, lead time, appearance, strength or fit.

## Typical commercial use cases

- pilot runs and pre-production prototypes
- enclosures, brackets, mounts, spacers and functional components
- jigs, fixtures and workshop tooling
- replacement parts and short-run manufacturing
- marketing/demo models and display samples
- high-detail resin parts (masters, stamps, small precision components)

Not sure which process fits best? We’ll recommend the right route (**FDM vs resin**) based on your part’s real constraints and intended use.

## Quality and consistency workflow

We keep commercial jobs predictable by agreeing the essentials upfront:
- material choice and finish expectations
- critical dimensions and acceptable variation
- quantity, timeline and packaging requirements
- revision/version control for repeatable reorders

Each batch includes visual QC, and where needed we measure agreed critical features using precision callipers. If your project benefits from documentation, we can provide a simple measurement report (scope agreed per project).

For tighter fit requirements, we can validate the design through test prints and calibration iterations before scaling up to the full batch.

---

## First-article samples (recommended for larger batches)

For larger orders, we can produce a **first-article / sample run** before the full batch. This helps confirm fit, finish and functional behaviour early, reducing risk and avoiding expensive surprises at scale. Terms and scope are agreed upfront based on the project.

Depending on your needs, we can quote:

## Ordering process

1) Send files + brief (use case, quantity, deadline, critical dimensions, finish expectations).  
2) We confirm feasibility, recommended material/process, lead time, and packaging/QC options.  
3) If needed, we run a first-article sample for approval.  
4) We produce, QC-check, package and ship — with support for repeat orders and revisions.

## What to include in an RFQ (to get an accurate quote fast)

To quote quickly and accurately, please include:
- **Files** (stl/step/3mf/obj) + any notes on what must not change
- **Quantity** (and whether you expect repeat reorders)
- **Deadline** (ideal and latest acceptable delivery date)
- **Material preference** (or the part’s use case so we can recommend)
- **Finish expectations** (cosmetic vs functional, visible faces, support-mark sensitivity)
- **Critical dimensions / fit requirements** (holes, mating interfaces, clearances)
- **Colour** (if important) and any branding/labeling needs
- **Packaging requirements** (bulk, individual bagging, labels, kitting)
- **QC expectations** (visual only vs measured features; measurement report needed or not)
- **Delivery address / shipping preference** (UK or international)

If you’re unsure about material or settings, describe the use case and we’ll propose options with clear trade-offs (unit cost vs lead time vs appearance vs fit).

## Reorders and version control

For commercial work, consistency matters. We can support repeat orders by keeping a confirmed spec for each part, including:
- revision/version (e.g., Rev A / Rev B) and change notes
- material + colour
- key print parameters that affect output consistency (process, layer height, nozzle, orientation/support approach where applicable)
- finishing and packaging requirements
- agreed QC scope (critical dimensions to measure and acceptable variation)

When you request a reorder, you can reference the part name/ID and revision, and we’ll match the confirmed spec. If anything needs to change, we’ll confirm the new revision before production.
