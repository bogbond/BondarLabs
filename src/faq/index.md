## Getting a quote

### How do I get a quote?
Use the **[quote form]({{ '/quote/' | url }})** and upload your file(s). If you don’t have a file, you can still request a quote with a short brief (what the part needs to do), photos/sketches and key dimensions.

### What file formats do you accept?
We accept **stl, step, 3mf and obj**. If you have a native CAD file (STEP or project files), edits are typically faster and more accurate than working from STL.

### I don’t know which material to choose — can you help?
Yes. Tell us how the part will be used (strength, heat exposure, flexibility, surface finish, indoors/outdoors) and we’ll recommend the best option.

### Can you review my file before printing?
Yes — we can check printability and flag typical issues (fragile walls, tight clearances, support-sensitive geometry) before production.

## FDM 3D printing (filament)

### What is FDM best for?
FDM is ideal for **functional parts**, enclosures, brackets, mounts, jigs/fixtures, prototypes and small-batch production.

### What’s the maximum size you can print?
Single-piece FDM prints can be produced up to **420 × 420 × 480 mm** depending on geometry and orientation.  
If you need something larger, parts can be **split into sections** and printed separately, with alignment features for clean assembly (bonding/assembly can be quoted if needed).

### What layer heights and nozzles do you use?
Typical layer heights are **0.16–0.20 mm** (with options from **0.06–0.30 mm** depending on the goal).  
Standard nozzles are **0.2 mm** and **0.4 mm**; **0.6 mm** and **0.8 mm** are available on request for faster production and more robust walls.

### What materials do you offer for FDM?
Common options include **PLA, PETG, ABS, ASA, Nylon, TPU**, and **carbon-filled PLA**. If you’re unsure, we’ll recommend the best match based on the real use case.

### Do you offer many colours?
Yes — PLA has a wide in-stock colour range. PETG has a core colour range. Engineering materials (ABS/ASA/Nylon/TPU) are typically stocked in practical colours (black/white/natural), with sourcing available on request.

### How accurate are FDM parts?
Accuracy depends on geometry, material and orientation. For well-designed parts, we can achieve **high dimensional consistency (up to ±0.2%)**.  
If your project needs a tight fit, we can run **test prints and measurement-led iterations** until the fit is right (agreed upfront).

## Resin 3D printing (MSLA)

### When should I choose resin over FDM?
Resin is best when you need **very fine detail**, smooth surfaces and crisp small features — for miniatures, figurines, master models, stamps/seals and small precision components.

### What resin print quality do you offer?
Typical layer heights are **0.05 mm** (standard), **0.03 mm** (fine detail) and **0.10 mm** (draft).

### What resins are available?
We stock **water-washable resin** as a default option (grey recommended for consistency), plus **standard**, **ABS-like**, and **flexible resin** on request (geometry dependent).

### Why do you recommend grey resin?
Grey resins typically provide the best dimensional consistency. **White and clear** can require additional tuning when needed (because light behaves differently during exposure).

### Are resin parts shipped “ready to use”?
Yes — resin orders include a full workflow as standard: **support removal → thorough washing/cleaning → controlled drying → final UV curing → QC + safe packaging**.

### Can you hollow resin parts to reduce cost?
Yes — **by agreement**. For larger models we can propose hollowing, drainage holes and a cleaning approach that ensures internal cavities are properly washed and dried. We’ll explain the trade-offs and confirm with you before proceeding.

## Lead times & scheduling

### How long does a typical order take?
Small orders are typically **2–3 business days**. Urgent jobs can sometimes ship **the same day** if timing allows.

### What about larger batches?
For batches (e.g., **50–500+ parts**), lead times are often **3–7 days** depending on part complexity, settings and finishing. We confirm timelines before production.

### Do you offer first-article samples for batches?
Yes — we can produce a **first-article / sample run** before the full batch, which is recommended for larger commercial orders.

## Shipping, delivery & collection

### Do you ship across the UK?
Yes — UK shipping is standard and sent **with tracking**.

### Which couriers do you use?
Typically **Royal Mail, Evri, and DPD**. In special cases, we can use **UPS or Yodel**. We’ll choose a balanced option by default (reputation/time/price), and you can request a specific service if you prefer.

### Can I choose an express option?
Yes — options like **Royal Mail Special Delivery (by 1pm)** or **DPD Next Day** can be arranged on request.

### How is shipping priced?
Shipping is charged **separately** and billed **at courier cost** (no markup). Price depends on packed size, weight and service level.

### Do you offer same-day local delivery?
If timing allows, yes — same-day delivery by car can be arranged locally (Cambridge/Huntingdon/Peterborough direction). Availability depends on workload, print completion time and distance.

### Can I collect locally?
Yes — collection is available **by appointment** in **Brampton, Huntingdon (PE28 4SU area)**. Exact pickup details are shared after confirming a time.

### Do you ship internationally?
Yes — international shipping is available on request and is sent with tracking (commonly via Royal Mail or UPS depending on the job).

### Who pays customs/import fees?
Import taxes, VAT and customs fees are the buyer’s responsibility. If prepayment is required for your destination, we’ll confirm it with you before dispatch.

### How are parts packaged?
Parts are packed to match the job. Small items may ship in padded mailers to keep shipping affordable; larger or fragile prints are boxed and protected. We also reuse clean, strong packaging from 3D printing supplies where appropriate (a practical upcycling approach).

### What if my parcel is lost or damaged?
If a tracked parcel hasn’t arrived within **10 working days** after dispatch, we’ll offer either a **full refund (including shipping)** or a **reprint and reship**.  
If items arrive damaged, contact us with photos (box + items) and we’ll agree a fair solution — replacement or refund.

## Finishing, QC and business orders

### What finishing is included by default?
FDM: sensible print settings + basic support removal + safe packaging.  
Resin: support removal + washing/cleaning + controlled drying + UV curing + safe packaging.

### What optional services can you do?
Depending on the project: sanding/clean-up, bonding/assembly, heat-set brass inserts, providing bolts, priming/painting, individual bagging/labels/kitting, NFC embedding, and measurement reporting.

### Can you provide measurement reports?
Yes — by agreement. We can provide annotated measurement points, photos showing how dimensions are measured, and a table (Excel/CSV). Where relevant, we can include a comparison vs CAD and deviation (%).

### Do you support commercial orders?
Yes. We can support small and mid-size business orders, repeat reorders with revision tracking, and first-article samples where needed. See **[Batch & Commercial Orders]({{ '/services/batch-production/' | url }})**.

### Can you sign an NDA?
Yes — confidentiality can be agreed upfront, and we can sign a customer-provided NDA where required.

## Design & file support

### Can you help if I don’t have a perfect file?
Yes — we can assist with practical file prep and CAD-based adjustments so parts print reliably and fit as intended. See **[3D Design & File Support]({{ '/services/design-support/' | url }})**.

### Do you offer 3D scanning or organic character sculpting?
Not currently. Our design support focuses on **parametric CAD and geometry-based parts** (enclosures, brackets, mounts, spacers, functional components). If you already have an organic model file, we can prepare it for printing and produce it as a print job.
